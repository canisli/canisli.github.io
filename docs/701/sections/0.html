  • Groups
    • Subgroup of direct product is not necessarily a direct product of subgroups
    • Subgroups of index 2 are normal. More generally, Subgroups of index 𝑝 where 𝑝 is the smallest prime dividing |𝐺| are normal.
    • For distinct primes 𝑝 and 𝑞, ℤ𝑝𝑞≅ℤ𝑝×ℤ𝑞.
  • Conjugacy classes
    • Conjugacy classes for abelian group e.g. 𝐶𝑛: Each element forms its own class
    • Conjugation can be thought of relabelling, e.g. change of basis and conjugacy classes of 𝑆𝑛 having same structure.
    • Chance that two randomly chosen elements commute: 𝐶𝐺|𝐺|≤58 where 𝐶𝐺 is the number of conjugacy classes.1
  • Homomorphisms
    • Image is not necessarily normal.
    • |𝜑(𝑔)| divides |𝑔|.
    • Commutation and center is preserved.
    • |ker𝜑||im𝜑|=|𝐺|
  • Symmetric group
    • Generated by a 2 cycle and an 𝑛 cycle (equivalent to set of transpositions of adjacent elements).
    • Conjugacy classes of 𝑆𝑛 have the same cycle structure
    • Center: 𝑍(𝑆𝑛) is trivial for 𝑛≥3.
    • Only subgroup of 𝑆𝑛 with index 2 (order 𝑛!/2) is 𝐴𝑛.
  • Alternating group
    • 𝐴𝑛,𝑛≥3 is generated by the set of 3-cycles.
    • Center: 𝑍(𝐴𝑛) is trivial for 𝑛≥4.
    • 𝐴4 is not simple. 𝐴𝑛,𝑛≥5 is simple.
      • Cor 𝐴𝑛 is the only nontrivial normal subgroup of 𝑆𝑛 for 𝑛≥5.
    • Prop 𝐴4 does not have a subgroup of order 6, since normal subgroups are unions of conjugcy classes including 𝑒.
    • Conjugacy classes: See Section 5.4
