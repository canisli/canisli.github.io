3.5. Continuity

  • Lem (Continuity of roots) Suppose 𝑝 is a monic polynomial of degree 𝑛 and {𝑝𝑘} is a sequence of monic polynomials of degree ≤𝑛. Then
    1. If the roots of 𝑝𝑘 approach that of 𝑝, then 𝑝𝑘→𝑝
    2. If 𝑝𝑘→𝑝, then the roots of 𝑝𝑘 can be numbered so that they approach those of 𝑝.
  • Prop Let 𝐴 be an 𝑛×𝑛 complex matrix.
    1. There is a sequence of diagonalizable matrices 𝐴𝑘 approaching 𝐴. That is, diagonal matrices are dense in ℂ𝑛×𝑛.
    2. The sequence of characteristic polynomials of 𝑝𝑘 approaches that of 𝑝.
    3. The roots (eigenvalues) of the 𝑝𝑘 can be numered so that they approach those of 𝑝.
