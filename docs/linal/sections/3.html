Proposition 6. Suppose 𝐴𝑛𝑣=𝜆𝑣 for a square matrix 𝐴 and 𝑣≠0, not necessarily diagonalizable. Then an nth root of 𝜆 is an eigenvalue of 𝐴.
Proof
Write the Jordan normal form of 𝐴, and invoke the fact that the eigenvalues are entries on the diagonal.

□

Remark. Eigenvectors are not unique. The projector onto the eigenspace is the more faithful object: it uniquely represents the subspace without any of these arbitrary choices.
