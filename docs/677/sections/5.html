5. Moment Generating Functions

MGFs are useful in calculating moments, addressing combinatorics problems, characterizing the distribution of the sum of a random number of independent random variables.

MGFs play a central role in the study of brancing processes, large deviations theory (asymptotics of ℙ(𝑋≥𝑐)), the bridge between complex analysis and probability, and limit theorems.
