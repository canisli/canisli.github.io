Definition 53. A field 𝔽 is a set with two laws of compositiion called addition and multiplication such that
  1. 𝔽 equipped with addition is an abelian group with identity denoted as 0.
  2. Multiplication is commutative. 𝔽\{0} equipped with multiplication is an abelian group with identity denoted as 1.
  3. Distributive law holds.
Definition 54. A vector space 𝑉 over a field 𝔽 is a set with addition 𝑉×𝑉→𝑉 and scalar multiplication 𝔽×𝑉→𝑉 defined such that
  • 𝑉 equipped with addition is an abelian group with identity denoted as 0.
  • Scalar multiplication is associative and distributive over addition.
  • 1𝑣=𝑣 for all 𝑣 where 1∈𝔽.
Definition 55 (Morphisms).
  • A homomorphism is a map between two groups that preserves the group operation.
  • An isomorphism is a bijective homomorphism.
  • An automorphism is an isomorphism from 𝐺 to itself.
  • An endomorphism is a homomorphism from 𝐺 to itself.
