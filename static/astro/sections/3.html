  • Newtonian cosmology: 2nd order IVP

    2. Initial velocities: Hubble’s law: 𝐯𝑖=𝐻(𝑡=0)𝐫𝑖
    3. Time evolution ODE: Newton’s law
    • Conservation of energy gives Friedmann equation
    • The result is that everything expands by the same scale factor, independent of 𝑅max.
