11. Approximation

  • For a minimization problem 𝒫︀, an algorithm is an 𝛼-approximation algorithm for 𝒫︀ if it yields at most 𝛼 times the optimal answer. For maximization it yields at least 1/𝛼 times the optimal answer. In both cases 𝛼≥1, and the goal is to bring it down as close as possible.
